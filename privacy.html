Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Pixel Office is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage their own YouTube channel. It runs on the owner's own computer.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), Pixel Office uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel and set their title, description, tags and publish time;
  • read the status of those videos;
  • read the channel's own view and retention statistics.

How data is stored

Login tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Pixel Office does not collect data about viewers or any other person.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's local files removes all stored data.

Pixel Office's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in (consent) screen for Pixel Office.

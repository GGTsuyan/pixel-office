Pixel Office

A personal video automation tool.

Pixel Office is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps one creator make short animated videos for their own YouTube channel and schedule them for publishing.

What it does with Google / YouTube

  • Uploads videos the owner has approved to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Sets the title, description, tags and scheduled publish time of those videos.
  • Reads the status and view statistics of the channel's own videos, to choose better posting times.

It is not a public service. It is used only by its owner, on their own channel.
